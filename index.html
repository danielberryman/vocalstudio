I

Approach

Function first. Artistry follows.

I'm Daniel, a voice teacher and a working tenor. I teach two things I wish I'd been taught sooner. First, how to listen to the great old singers and hear what they did. Second, how to feel your own voice from the inside.

Most students feel the change in the first lesson.

  • If you can hear it, you can learn to make it.Listening comes before singing.
  • If you can feel it, you can make it again and again.Feeling is how a sound becomes yours.
  • No shortcuts.A new habit in the body takes weeks, not minutes.
  • One person has many voices.Not just one.

Opera

Viennese Opera Ball

The thinnest folds, fully closed but never pressed. The larynx a little low, a lot of space.

Broadway

Les Misérables · The Fantasticks

Less of everything than opera, except the closure and the twang. Those stay.

Pop & Rock

Beatles & Paul Simon with symphonies

A higher larynx, less spread in the jaw, no tilt even up high.

The engine stays the same in every style. Style is how you set the rest.
